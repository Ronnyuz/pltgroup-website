FAQ

The short version

Didn’t find your answer? Message us on Telegram — we reply during business hours.

How much does a project cost?

Each service shows a starting price. We give an exact price after a short call and fix it in the contract — it won’t change without your agreement.

How does payment work?

We work under contract: 50% upfront, 50% after acceptance. Payment by bank transfer in UZS at the Central Bank rate on the invoice date.

How long does it take?

A landing page takes 1–2 weeks, a bot 2–4 weeks, CRM and analytics 3–6 weeks. Timelines also depend on how quickly we receive materials.

What if I need changes after launch?

Bug fixes are covered by a 30-day warranty. New features are handled through a support plan or at an hourly rate.

Who owns the website and code?

You do. After payment we hand over the source code and access to hosting, domain and services.

Do you work outside Tashkent?

Yes. We work remotely across Uzbekistan and with companies abroad. Meetings in Tashkent or online.
