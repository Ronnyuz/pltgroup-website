Privacy policy

How PLT GROUP handles data of visitors to pltgroup.uz.

What data we receive

When you send the request form, we receive the name, contact and task description you entered. They are kept in a protected system that only PLT GROUP staff can access. Then WhatsApp opens with a ready message. The site collects no other personal data. If you write in the chat on the site, your messages are stored and sent to an artificial intelligence service that generates the consultant's replies. The name and contact you leave in the chat go into a request.

How we use it

Only to reply to your request and prepare a proposal. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, runs on Cloudflare and uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and collects anonymous visit statistics. The chat consultant's replies are generated by an artificial intelligence service. WhatsApp and Telegram apply their own rules.

Deleting data

Contact us through any channel on the site and we will delete our correspondence and data about your request.
