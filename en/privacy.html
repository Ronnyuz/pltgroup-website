Privacy policy

How PLT GROUP handles data of visitors to pltgroup.uz.

What data we receive

The site does not collect or store personal data. The request form builds a message in your browser and opens WhatsApp. We receive data only after you send that message yourself.

How we use data

Your name, contact and task description are used only to reply to your request and prepare a proposal. We do not sell or share them with third parties.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you move to WhatsApp or Telegram, their rules apply.

Deleting data

To delete our correspondence and data about your request, contact us through any channel listed on the site.
