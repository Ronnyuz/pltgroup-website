Privacy policy

How PLT GROUP handles the data of visitors to its websites.

What data we receive

When you send a request form on the PLT GROUP website or the PLT Digital, PLT Agrovia and PLT Retail websites, we receive the name, phone or Telegram, the selected option and the comment you entered. The websites do not collect any other personal data.

How we use it

Only to reply to your request and prepare an offer. The request is stored in a secure system that only PLT GROUP staff can access, and it is sent to their work email. We do not sell or share data with third parties for their own purposes.

Third-party services

Request emails are sent by FormSubmit. The websites run on Cloudflare, load Google Fonts and use Google Analytics, which sets cookies and collects anonymous visit statistics. Your language and theme choice is stored only in your browser. WhatsApp and Telegram have their own rules.

Deleting data

Write to info@pltgroup.uz or call +998 33 111 30 10 and we will delete the data about your request.
